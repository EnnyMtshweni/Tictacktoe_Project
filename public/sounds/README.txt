SOUND FILES — drop your own audio files here
=============================================

The game expects three MP3 files in this folder:

  move.mp3  — a soft tap or click sound, played when any square is claimed
  win.mp3   — a short cheerful jingle, played when a player wins
  draw.mp3  — a gentle descending tone, played when the game ends in a draw

You can use any royalty-free audio. Good free sources:
  https://freesound.org
  https://opengameart.org
  https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/

Keep files short (under 2 seconds for move, under 4 seconds for win/draw)
for the best feel. Vite will serve them as-is from /public/sounds/.
